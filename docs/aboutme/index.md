#About Me


My name is Rebekah Smith, and I am a mechanical engineering student interested in solving problems, designing systems, and understanding how different areas of engineering work together. I chose mechanical engineering because of its broad range of applications and its connections to other areas of engineering, including civil and electrical engineering. I like that mechanical engineering gives me the opportunity to work with many different types of problems instead of being limited to one specific area. My experience as an aerodynamicsist on my FSAE team has given me the opportunity to apply engineering concepts to real projects. Through FSAE, I have worked with aerodynamic design and manufacturing and have learned how individual design decisions can affect the performance of an entire vehicle. This experience has helped me develop my problem-solving, design, and teamwork skills while giving me a better understanding of engineering outside of the classroom. As I continue my education, I want to become a well-rounded mechanical engineer who can approach problems from multiple perspectives and develop practical solutions.
I also believe that being an engineer means being able to explain and support the decisions I make. To me, defending an engineering decision means showing the process behind my thinking and providing the data, calculations, testing, or other evidence that supports my final decision. I believe I could defend an engineering decision because I understand the importance of explaining not only what decision was made, but also why it was chosen. However, I know that I can improve how clearly and thoroughly I communicate my reasoning, and I hope to develop this skill throughout the semester. This assignment took approximately 7 hours to complete and gave me an opportunity to begin thinking about the standards I want to hold myself to as I continue developing as an engineer and preparing for future engineering projects and career opportunities.
